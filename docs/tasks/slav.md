5. **Plans, structure and traps**: the "Slav triangle" c6-d5-e6, the light-squared bishop development *before* ...e6, queenside play, what to do when White grabs space with e4, typical 1100 mistakes (…e6 too early locking the bishop, …Nf6 blunders to Ne5/Qb3, forgetting to play …Bf5/…Bg4) and real traps (name only if sure).
